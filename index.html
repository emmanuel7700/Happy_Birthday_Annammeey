How I Met Annamma ❤️

From one small smile over a Physics answer sheet… to someone I never want to lose.

12th Standard • The beginning

📄 A Physics Answer Sheet

I first saw you when I went to distribute the Physics test answer sheets. At that time, I thought your name was Annlee. Then I realised I had got it completely wrong. 😭 But when you received the answer sheet, you smiled. It was such a small moment, but that smile gave me a strange curiosity to find out who you were and what your real name was.

The very next day

🌸 Deepthy Miss & That Familiar Face

The next day, I saw you coming to school with Deepthy Miss, who taught me Physics in 11th and was one of my favourite teachers. After that, I began noticing you more often. You always seemed to come to class with a pleasant face and a kind, positive energy. And somehow I still remember that most of the time, you had a spiral book in your hand. 😂

Slowly...

👀 My Attention Fell on You

Gradually I noticed that you were talkative, and I genuinely loved that character. Your energy, the way you spoke, your pleasant face and the kind of person you seemed to be kept pulling my attention towards you. I finally asked one of my friends about you. He simply said, “Aval oru paavam, nalla koch aanu.” Somewhere along the way, my heart had already fallen for you.

Still in 12th

🤐 I Chose to Wait

I shared everything with my close friends, and naturally they told me to tell you. But I knew it wasn’t the right time. We were in 12th, and I never wanted my feelings to become something that shifted your mind away from your studies. So even though I wanted to tell you, I decided to wait.

March 19 • After board exams

⏳ Life Moved On

Board exams ended on March 19. Then came entrance exams, allotments and college. Once I finally settled into first year, I felt like checking your Instagram profile. But I couldn’t find it. My brain immediately convinced me that maybe you somehow knew everything and had blocked me. 😭 I was genuinely disappointed.

Second year

📲 One Follow Request

Then, in second year, I got a follow request from you. It was just a notification, but my heart was ridiculously happy. And of course, the whole discussion with my friends started again.

One Instagram story

🇦🇷 The Argentina Jersey

One day you posted a story wearing an Argentina jersey, and I replied. I normally like and reply to my friends’ stories too, so replying itself wasn’t unusual. But this one was different. This time, I actually wanted a conversation to begin. I wanted to talk to you. So I asked about your college, your vacation and little things. The jersey was really just my excuse to finally start talking to you. 😂

Semester break • Munnar

😂 The Plan That Failed

Long before that, my friends had already drafted a plan. During our semester break, we went to Munnar and finally decided it was time to text you. We used the number you had in 12th standard… only to realise later that you weren’t using that number anymore. After all that planning, the mission failed and the plan was postponed once again. 😂

July 13, 2026 ❤️

The Day I Finally Told You

Finally came July 13. We were on a group call, and my friends gave me an amazing opportunity. I confessed to you. I wasn’t even most afraid of whether your answer would be yes or no; I was more afraid of how you would react. I was really nervous. But the way you handled it stunned me. You were calm and mature, and instead of making the moment awkward, you tried your best to make me comfortable. That moment made me respect you even more.

From that day onwards...

📞 Then We Started Talking

From that day, we began talking daily. Small conversations became long calls, random stories became things I looked forward to, and somewhere between all of that, a bond was created. And that bond is still going on. I genuinely love your company, Annamma.

I want the same.
I want you in my life forever.

You are one of the rarest gems I have met in my entire life. ❤️

And our story is still being written… ♾️
